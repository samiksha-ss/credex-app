# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

## Target User
**Title:** Director of Engineering, VP of Engineering, or Startup Founder / CTO.
**Stage:** Series A to Series C startups (50-250 employees).
**Why:** At this stage, startups have scaled headcount fast enough that software bloat occurs, but they are suddenly feeling pressure from their board/CFO to extend runway and demonstrate capital efficiency. They are large enough to have $5k-$20k/mo in AI spend, making the Credex value proposition meaningful.

## The Trigger Moment
What are they doing right before they want this tool?
- Reviewing their monthly Ramp/Brex statement and seeing a massive spike in "OpenAI API" or "Anthropic" spend.
- Preparing for a board meeting focused on runway extension.
- Searching Google for "Cursor vs Copilot enterprise pricing" or "How to monitor ChatGPT team usage."

## Where They Hang Out
- **Slack Communities:** CTO School, Rands Leadership Slack, YC Alumni Slack.
- **Subreddits:** r/SaaS, r/startups, r/devops.
- **X (Twitter):** Following accounts like Gergely Orosz (Pragmatic Engineer), Lenny Rachitsky, or VCs tweeting about capital efficiency.

## Acquiring the First 100 Users in 30 Days ($0 Budget)
"Post on Twitter" is not a strategy. Here is the specific playbook:
1. **The "Hall of Shame" Open Source List:** Create a viral GitHub repository titled "AI Pricing Secrets 2026." Document the obscure pricing tiers and hidden minimums of major AI tools. Link to the Credex Audit tool as the automated way to check if a startup is falling into these traps. Post this repo to Hacker News; developer tools related to pricing transparency regularly hit the front page.
2. **Cold Emailing Fractional CFOs:** Don't target the CTOs directly at first. Target Fractional CFOs who serve Series A/B startups. Give them the tool for free. They look like heroes to their clients by cutting costs, and they act as a distribution channel bringing multiple companies into the Credex funnel.
3. **The "Roast My Stack" X Campaign:** Offer free, public "teardowns" of AI stacks. Ask founders to DM a screenshot of their AI billing page, and reply publicly with a visual breakdown generated by the Credex tool. The visual nature of the report makes it highly shareable.

## The Unfair Distribution Channel
Credex’s unfair advantage is **aggregated proprietary data**. As audits are completed, Credex builds the only real-time dataset of what startups are *actually* paying for AI tools versus retail pricing. This data can be packaged into a quarterly "State of AI Spend" report. Journalists and industry newsletters will cite this report, driving high-DR backlinks and continuous organic traffic to the audit tool.

## Week-1 Traction Metrics
If this works, by the end of Week 1 we should see:
- 1,500 unique visitors to the landing page.
- 300 completed audits (20% conversion rate from visitor to completion).
- 45 email captures / leads generated (15% conversion rate from audit completion).
- 5 high-intent "Consultation Booked" requests.

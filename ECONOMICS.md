# Unit Economics

## The Value of a Converted Lead
To understand the economics of the AI Spend Audit tool, we first need to value a converted lead for Credex's core business (selling discounted AI infrastructure credits).
- **Average Customer:** A Series A/B startup spending ~$10,000/mo on AI infra (OpenAI, Anthropic, Cursor).
- **Credex Value Prop:** 20% discount via aggregated credits = $2,000/mo savings for the startup.
- **Credex Margin:** Assuming Credex buys credits at a 30% discount and sells at a 20% discount, the margin is 10%.
- **Revenue per Customer:** 10% of $10,000 = $1,000/mo in gross profit.
- **Customer Lifespan:** Estimated at 24 months.
- **LTV (Lifetime Value):** $24,000.

Given standard B2B SaaS metrics, a marketing qualified lead (MQL) that books a consultation for an enterprise contract of this size is worth roughly **$500 to $1,000**.

## Estimated CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)
Based on the GTM plan, the initial distribution relies on high-leverage organic channels (Hacker News, Viral X teardowns, Fractional CFO partnerships).
- **Organic Social / HN:** $0 direct cost. The "cost" is engineering time to maintain the audit tool.
- **Fractional CFO Partnerships:** $0 direct cost, though potentially involves a revenue share (e.g., 10% of year 1 revenue), which acts as a variable cost rather than an upfront CAC.
- **Blended CAC target:** Given the viral nature of the tool, the blended CAC should remain extremely low, likely under **$150** per booked consultation in the first 6 months.

## Conversion Funnel Math
To achieve profitability on the engineering effort invested in this tool, the funnel needs to look roughly like this:
1. **Traffic:** 10,000 unique visitors per month.
2. **Audit Completion:** 20% conversion = 2,000 audits run.
3. **Lead Capture (Email provided):** 10% of audits = 200 emails captured.
4. **Consultation Booked (High intent):** 15% of captured emails = 30 booked calls.
5. **Closed Won (Credit Purchase):** 25% close rate = 7.5 new customers/mo.

At 7.5 new customers per month, generating $1,000/mo gross profit each, the tool adds **$7,500 in new MRR every month**. Over 12 months, that compounds to **$90,000 in MRR** (ignoring churn), effectively building a $1M ARR business solely from this lead generation engine.

## Driving $1M ARR in 18 Months
For this tool to drive $1M ARR in 18 months, the following must be true:
1. **The Market Matures:** Startups must transition from "growth at all costs" to "capital efficiency," making cost-audits a top priority for CFOs.
2. **The Output remains High Value:** The audit recommendations must remain incredibly sharp and accurate, ensuring that the tool isn't dismissed as a generic marketing calculator.
3. **Viral Coefficient:** The "Share Report" feature must achieve a viral coefficient above 0.2 (every 5 users bring in 1 new user via shared links to investors or co-founders), dramatically lowering CAC and scaling traffic beyond linear marketing efforts.
